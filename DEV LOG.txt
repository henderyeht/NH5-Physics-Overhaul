Physics Overhaul
Creation and development log
https://github.com/henderyeht/NH5-Physics-Overhaul

Author: henderyeht
Dates: 17 October 2026 through 19 October 2026

This is a NASCAR Heat 5 utility. It writes a sim-style tire, draft, and chassis set into Assembly-CSharp.dll for the game folder you pick. It is not a port of another game. Remove puts the saved values back. Backups stay outside the game. Other mods, Mod Workshop, and the driving-aid menu are left alone.

The runnable package is Physics Overhaul.zip in this repository. Unzip it. The folder contains SimHandling.exe, Mono.Cecil.dll, and READ ME.txt. Double-click SimHandling.exe. Close Heat 5 first. The dropdown lists full folder paths. Add build picks another install. Apply writes the patch. Remove puts it back.

Command Center has the same patch inside its own program. That project is not in this repository.


What the program is allowed to touch

The patch finds one float stored immediately before a field write, and replaces those four bytes. The file size stays the same. It does not rewrite the whole DLL.

Series covered, when that chassis exists in the build:
- Cup
- Cup DLC, skipped if that chassis is missing
- Xfinity
- Truck
- Dirt, banked, flat, and road
- Eldora truck

Tracks do not replace the tire. They add a grip and stiffness multiplier on top of it, plus the assists this patch turns off.

Left for the game:
- Driving aid menu. Expert stays raw. Hard, Normal, and Easy still add help on top of this physics.
- Garage. Spring, camber, pressure, brake bias, wedge, and the sway-bar ranges still mean what they meant.
- Tire wear, including the competition wear cut.
- Draft strength menu. Competition still forces that setting lower.
- Engine Modifier grip. Axle friction is left at the stock numbers, so the engine slider is still a percent of stock grip and it overwrites only those friction numbers.


17 October 2026. The utility

The first build was a window in the same style as the other Heat 5 tools. It finds installs, refuses mod folders and the multiplayer revival folder, and remembers folders you add. Apply and Remove work on the DLL in the folder you pick. A copy of the original floats is kept outside the game, so a later Apply does not treat an already patched file as stock.

The notes were rewritten the same day. They list which values change. They do not list amounts, they do not describe how the car rotates, and they do not read as a tire-only mod.


18 October 2026. Grip, assists, tires, draft

An early pass raised tire friction and downforce. On track that planted the car and made it easier. Both were written back to the stock numbers. Downforce is still stock. Friction is still stock: 1.2, 1.1, and 1.1 on the asphalt axle.

Built-in track assists were turned off: extra grip, steering help, racing line help, brake help, wall help, and stability help. The driving-aid menu was not zeroed, so the difficulty settings still add or remove help.

The tire curve was then given a real slip peak. Cup slicks make their cornering force at about 2 to 4 degrees of slip, then give force up once they slide. The first curve peaked at 4 degrees and gave up only a little in a full slide. Corner grip was still the track bonus, 1.47 on speedways, road courses, and short tracks.

That still did not feel like enough of a change, so corner grip and tire stiffness were brought back toward the neutral 1 the car uses before a track adds its bonus. Superspeedways and Bristol dirt kept the game's own proportion. The slide was made much deeper. Driving that build, the draft and the tire both felt too far, so they were eased:

- Corner grip, stiffness, and slide loss moved a quarter of the way back toward the milder curve.
- Draft width and draft strength moved halfway back from the wide bubble toward stock.
- Draft length stayed at 3 car lengths. Published tows are strong inside one length and still present out to about three.

Aero research from the same day, and what the game cannot store:

- The car behind in dirty air loses front downforce and gets tight. The lead car can go loose when another car unloads the spoiler. SAE wake work measured high turbulence and a large speed deficit in the tow.
- Side draft is a drag trade beside the leader, strongest about half to three-quarters of a length back and in a small side gap. The leader slows. The trailer still gains some.
- Close to the wall, the forces change by a large amount. This game has no wall-suction field. The wall assist that was turned off was a helper that pushed the car off the wall.
- Oil and water climb on the pushing car only, after a long time in the tow. This game has no clock for time spent in the draft. Heat numbers that can be edited apply the whole run, including the leader in clean air, so they were left alone.
- A bump uses the same contact force. The tire gives up more once the car is already sliding, so the same hit is harder to catch.
- Front and rear downforce were not shifted. A fixed shift would loosen or tighten the car in clean air. The published hook is the lead car in traffic, and the car behind gets tight. This game has one draft bubble for all of that.


19 October 2026. Weight and braking

The car felt heavy, and a hard brake lock did not make the car tight in the turn.

Cup mass in the game is 3500 lb. A Cup car is about 3400 lb dry and about 3650 lb with the driver, so the mass was left alone. The center height was the low number. The game stores 9 next to a 110 inch wheelbase. A published Winston Cup model on that wheelbase uses 17 inches. A low center transfers less weight, so the car stays planted and the nose does not load when you brake.

Center height was scaled by 17/9. Cup, Xfinity, Truck, and Cup DLC go from 9 to 17. Dirt banked and dirt flat go from 8.5 to 16.06. Dirt road goes from 5.5 to 10.39. The Eldora truck goes from 10 to 18.89. Each series keeps the ratio the game already used.

Hard braking should use up the tire. At a full stop there is little grip left to steer. If the fronts lock, the wheel goes numb and the car pushes. If the rears lock, the car gets loose. A workable brake split starts near 54 to 56 percent on the front, so the fronts lock a fraction before the rears. That split is the garage brake bias, so it was not overwritten.

The game was still adding rotation while the brakes were on. That helper is now off on every track type. Brake help was already off. There is no separate combined-slip number. Lateral grip and brake grip stay separate fields.

The repository was made public the same day so the values can be edited.

How to get a different effect

Edit src/HandlingPatch.cs. Each line is:

F(chassis or Physics, method, field, new value, stock value, required)

The new value is what Apply writes. The stock value is what Remove writes when that install has no saved original. Required false means a missing chassis, such as Cup DLC or Bristol dirt, is skipped instead of failing the Apply.

After a value change, rebuild the Release program, copy SimHandling.exe into dist, and rebuild Physics Overhaul.zip from dist/SimHandling.exe, dist/Mono.Cecil.dll, and dist/READ ME.txt. Close Heat 5 and Apply again. If the patch was already on, Apply overwrites these fields and keeps the saved originals for Remove.

IsApplied looks for magic_c equal to 1.525 on the Cup axle. If you change that shape number, change the check in IsApplied to the same number or the window will say the patch is off.

Tire
- Raise corner grip (grip_lf and the other three) toward 1.47 and the car has more corner grip. Lower it toward 1.0 or below and the car slides sooner.
- Raise stiffness (stiff_lf and the other three) toward 1.2 and the tire builds force faster. Lower it and the tire feels softer.
- Keep the superspeedway and Bristol numbers in the same proportion as stock if those tracks should stay where the game put them. Plate grip ratio is 1.3/1.47. Plate stiffness ratio is 1.13/1.2. Bristol grip ratio is 1.5/1.47.
- Raise magic_c and a full slide gives up more force. Lower it toward 1.3 and the slide hangs on longer. If you change magic_c, recompute magic_b so the peak stays where you want it: magic_b = tan(pi / (2 * magic_c)) / peak degrees. Asphalt peak is 4. Dirt peak is 15. Use the same magic_c on asphalt and dirt. They share that number in the file.
- grip_fade is the share given up across the fade window. grip_fade_start and grip_fade_stop are the slip angles where that fade runs. A wider window makes the loss more gradual.

Draft
- Shorter max_draft_lon_carlen makes the tow die sooner. Longer makes it reach farther back. 3 is the outer range from the tow research.
- Smaller min_draft_width_carwid means full effect only when the cars are lined up. Larger means you can be offset and still get the full tow.
- Larger max_draft_width_carwid reaches a car farther to the side. 1.121 is just past a car on the door. 0.992 is the stock edge. 1.25 was the wider pass that was eased.
- Larger draft_influence is a stronger tow. 0.1575 is stock. 0.30 was the strong pass. 0.22875 is halfway between them. Competition and the draft menu still scale whatever you set.

Chassis
- Higher cm_height moves more weight to the nose under braking and to the outside tires in a corner. The car feels less planted. Lower, toward 9, is the heavy planted feel this build was changed away from.
- More negative front_lift is more front downforce. More negative rear_lift is more rear downforce. More front and less rear loosens the car everywhere, including clean air. More rear and less front tightens it everywhere. That is why the split was left at stock.
- Higher drag (the chassis drag field, not the assist drag) slows the car more. It does not create dirty air by itself.

Brakes
- arcade_brake_oversteer above 0 adds rotation while braking, which makes the entry loose. 0 leaves the tire to decide. Front lock then pushes. The garage brake bias still chooses which end locks first.
- Do not write brake_bias here. That is the garage slider.

Assists
- 0 is off. Put the stock number back on that line and that helper returns. The menu aids still stack on top of whatever base you leave.

Leave these alone unless you also change the matching tool
- frict_k1, frict_k2, and long_frict. Engine Modifier owns them and expects the stock numbers.
- Sway range, spring range, camber range, toe, and anti-pitch. The garage owns the setup inside those ranges.
- min_wear_grip. Competition multiplies it. The patch does not.


What this game still cannot do

- Take front downforce off only the car in the wake.
- Raise the leader's drag when someone is beside him, separate from the tow.
- Pull a car into the wall.
- Wait to heat the oil, water, brakes, or tires until the car has been in the tow.
- Change the hit impulse. The tire only changes how hard the slide is to catch after the hit.
